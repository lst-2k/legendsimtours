Format 01 · Pairs
Alternate Hole

"Two players, one mission — you cover the odds, I'll take the evens."

🔄
Split the course Player 1 plays all odd holes (1, 3, 5 … 17). Player 2 plays all even holes (2, 4, 6 … 18). Each plays exactly 9 holes. Players are then reversed evens become odds, odds become evens, 1 week, 1 card, 2 results.
🏆
One ball, one result Only the designated player's score counts on their holes. Lower gross score wins the hole for the team.
🤝
Team identity The pair's combined performance determines the match result — communication and course management are everything.
⚡
Captains pick pairings Each week, captains choose who pairs with whom. Strong on odds + strong on evens = a formidable combination.
Matchplay scoring Win a hole: +1 · Halve: 0 · Lose: -1 · Most holes wins the match

🏌️
Format 02 · Pairs
4 Ball Better Ball

"All four of us tee off — but only the best score on each side counts."

🎯
Everyone plays every hole All four players (two per team) play their own ball on every hole across all 18. No splitting — full rounds for everyone.
⭐
Best ball wins the hole Each team takes their lower score on the hole. Team A's best vs Team B's best — lowest gross wins it.
🛡️
Safety net format One bad hole doesn't sink the ship — your partner can bail you out. Encourages bold play since you have backup.
🔥
Hot streaks win matches If either player in a pair catches fire, the team benefits. One exceptional round can carry a match single-handedly.
Matchplay scoring Win a hole: +1 · Halve: 0 · Lose: -1 · Most holes wins the match
3
🎯
Format 03 · 1v1
Singles

"No partner to lean on. Just you, your opponent, and 18 holes."

👤
One vs one, pure and simple Individual players go head to head. Each player plays their own ball for all 18 holes — no pairs, no backup.
🕳️
Hole by hole battle Lower gross score on each hole wins it. Tie the hole, it's halved. The player who wins more holes wins the match.
📊
Double the matches In a singles week, each team fields one player per slot — so an 8-person squad produces 8 individual matches that week.
🧠
Mental strength tested There is nowhere to hide. Course management, composure under pressure, and consistency over 18 holes define who wins.
Matchplay scoring Win a hole: +1 · Halve: 0 · Lose: -1 · Most holes wins the match

🏆 How points are awarded

Every match result earns tournament points for the winning team. Matches are decided by holes won — not by total strokes.
